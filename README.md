# Last-Card
Last Card is a browser card game in the style of UNO. Play against three computer opponents and be the first to empty your hand. Match cards by color or number, use Skip, Reverse, Draw Two and Wild cards, and remember to call “Last card!” before your second-to-last play. Scores carry across rounds. Works on phone and desktop.

**Last Card** is a card game you play in your browser, in the style of UNO. You play against three computer opponents, Mira, Dev and Juno. It runs from a single HTML file and needs no download or account.

**The goal**
Be the first to get rid of all your cards. Everyone starts with 7. On your turn you play one card that matches the top card of the discard pile by color, number or symbol. If you can't play, you draw from the pile.

**The cards**
- **Number cards (0-9)** in four colors: red, yellow, green and blue.
- **Skip** makes the next player lose their turn.
- **Reverse** changes the direction of play.
- **Draw Two** makes the next player pick up 2 cards and lose their turn.
- **Wild** lets you choose the next color.
- **Wild Draw Four** lets you choose the color and makes the next player pick up 4 cards. You can only play it if you have no card of the current color.

**Calling last card**
When you have two cards left, press the **Last card!** button before you play one of them. If you forget, you have to draw 2 cards as a penalty. The computer players never forget.

**Scoring**
When a round ends, the winner gets points for the cards left in everyone else's hands. Number cards are worth their face value, Skip, Reverse and Draw Two are worth 20, and Wilds are worth 50. Your points carry over from round to round, and the results screen shows everyone's totals. Press **New game** to reset the scores.

**How it looks and plays**
- The table is dark green, with the three opponents across the top and the draw and discard piles in the middle.
- Your hand is along the bottom. Cards you can play are highlighted and lift up, and the rest are dimmed.
- A colored ring around the discard pile shows the current color.
- A message line tells you what every player just did.
- It works on both phones and desktops, and the cards overlap when you hold a lot of them.

**Not included**
Draw Two and Wild Draw Four can't be stacked on each other, and you can't challenge a Wild Draw Four. It's also a single-player game, with no online multiplayer.
